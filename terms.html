Joule — Terms and Conditions

Joule is a private, personal home-energy dashboard operated by its owner for non-commercial use.

Vehicle data authorised by the owner is used only to display and understand the owner's vehicle battery state and charging status alongside home-energy information.

Joule is not offered as a public service and does not sell, share, or commercially exploit vehicle data.

Access can be revoked by the vehicle/account owner at any time through the relevant Volvo account controls.
